Meet Caroline

Hello, I'm Caroline, founder of Hourglass Therapy. I am a qualified, integrative therapist, a registered member of the National Counselling & Psychotherapy Society (NCPS), and a certified Mental Health First Aider. I work with individuals and couples across the UK online and by phone, and in-person or outdoors in the Bristol area with nature-based "Walk and Talk" sessions.

About Caroline's Approach

As an integrative therapist, I tailor approaches from different therapeutic models to your specific needs. My approach is rooted in empathy, trust, and collaboration - offering a safe, compassionate, non-judgemental space where every aspect of your experience is welcome.

Outside the therapy room, I'm a parent to twins and bring a wealth of real-world experience from roles in the public and private sectors - media, education, police, technology, and law. These experiences enrich my client work, enabling me to relate to a broad spectrum of life challenges.

"Your journey is at the heart of what we do. Mutual respect, a genuine therapeutic relationship, and ethical practice underpin every session. I am here to sit alongside you, provide guidance, and offer practical tools as you discover new possibilities - always at your pace."

Professional Associations

MNCS Accredited Registrant

As an MNCS Accredited Registrant, I meet the high standards for safety and ethical practice set by the National Counselling and Psychotherapy Society.

Professional Standards Authority

This independent accreditation provides additional peace of mind, as our practice is held to high professional standards by a government-backed accredited register.

ACTO Level 1

As a member of ACTO, the leading organization for online therapy standards, we are committed to providing ethical and effective online counselling.

Mental Health First Aider

As a certified Mental Health First Aider, we promote a supportive environment by being trained to recognise and respond to mental health challenges.

Online & Telephone Counselling Certified

We are a Certified Online & Telephone Counsellor, demonstrating our professional training and competence in delivering services remotely.

Walk & Talk Therapy Trained

As a Walk & Talk trained therapist, we offer you the benefits of combining nature and physical activity with your counselling sessions.
