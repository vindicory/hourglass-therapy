Our Therapy Offerings

At Hourglass Therapy, we offer flexible and accessible support tailored to your needs – choose the approach that works best for you.

Face-to-Face Counselling

Meeting you in person and providing a safe, comfortable space to explore any challenges that stand in the way of your potential. Share, reflect, and grow in a confidential and supportive environment at our calm, welcoming Bristol location.

Book Now

Telephone Counselling

If you want to be heard but not seen visually. Your therapist will actively listen and work with your tone of voice and pace of speech. Connect by phone for talk therapy from the comfort of your own space – perfect for those who prefer not to be seen onscreen.

50 minutes UK-wide
Book Now

Online / Video Counselling

A way for you to engage in counselling via Zoom or Skype. We agree the time, place and pace to suit your counselling needs. Perfect for those who want face-to-face interaction from the comfort of their own home.

50 minutes UK-wide
Book Now

Walk & Talk Therapy

Gets us outside into the fresh air and nature. It can help improve mental wellbeing and can help people that feel socially isolated or lonely. Experience the healing benefits of the outdoors. Walking side by side in nature can offer new perspectives and is particularly supportive for those who find traditional settings overwhelming.

50 minutes Bristol area
Book Now

Couples Counselling

All relationships have ups and downs – let's talk in person and work through your issues together. Every relationship faces challenges. Together, we'll build communication, understanding, and stronger bonds in a warm, neutral setting.
