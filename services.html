Services

At Hourglass Therapy, we offer flexible and accessible support tailored to your needs - choose the approach that works best for you.

Available Therapy Services

Face-to-Face Counselling

Meet, reflect, and grow in a confidential and supportive environment at our calm, welcoming Bristol location.

Learn more

Telephone Counselling

Connect by phone for talk therapy from the comfort of your own space - perfect for those who prefer not to be seen onscreen.

Learn more

Online/Video Counselling

Meet virtually via Zoom or Skype. Flexible and accessible for busy lifestyles, enabling you to work on your goals wherever you are.

Learn more

Walk & Talk Therapy

Experience the healing benefits of the outdoors. Walking side by side in nature can offer new perspectives and is particularly supportive for those who find traditional settings overwhelming.

Learn more

Couples Counselling

Every relationship faces challenges. Together, we'll build communication, understanding, and stronger bonds in a warm, neutral setting.

Learn more

Hypnotherapy

A gentle and effective way to access the subconscious and facilitate positive change. Can help with anxiety, phobias, confidence, sleep issues, and smoking cessation.

Learn more

Eating Disorders Support

Sensitive, non-judgemental, and holistic support for eating disorders including anorexia, bulimia, binge eating, emotional eating, and body image issues.

Learn more

Supervision

Clinical supervision for trainee and qualified counsellors, both individually and in groups. A confidential space to reflect, grow, and receive guidance in line with best practice.

Learn more
