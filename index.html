Welcome

Your Journey to Wellbeing Starts Here

Welcome to Hourglass Therapy, a compassionate, Bristol-based Counselling and Psychotherapy practice. We offer a range of therapeutic approaches, including face-to-face, telephone, online, and Walk & Talk sessions, allowing support to fit seamlessly into your life.

Life is filled with challenges, transitions, and unexpected hurdles. Counselling can help you build a deeper self-awareness, nurture acceptance, and foster resilience as you move towards meaningful change and personal growth.

At Hourglass Therapy, you'll find a safe, supportive environment where your voice is heard, where your feelings matter, and where your individual journey is respected. Together, we'll explore your experiences, help you process difficult emotions, and work towards your unique goals.

Whether you're wrestling with anxiety, overcoming loss, struggling with self-esteem, or simply feeling stuck, Hourglass Therapy is here to guide you forward, at your own pace and in your own way.

About Me

Caroline

Hi, I'm Caroline, founder of Hourglass Therapy. I am a fully qualified counsellor and supervisor. I am a member of the National Counselling & Psychotherapy Society (NCPS), the National Hypnotherapy Society and a Mental Health First Aider. I provide online and telephone counselling throughout the UK as well as in-person and walk and talk therapy in the Bristol area.

I am an integrative therapist, which means I adapt my skills and knowledge to suit your individual needs. I offer a safe, compassionate, non-judgemental space where we can work together to help you move past the issues that stand in the way of your true potential.
