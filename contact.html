FAQ's

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I expect from my first session?

Your first session is an opportunity to meet, discuss what brings you to therapy, and explore how we might work together. It's a chance to ask questions and see if we're a good fit.

How many sessions will I need?

The number of sessions varies depending on your needs and goals. Some people benefit from short-term work (6-12 sessions), while others prefer longer-term support. We'll discuss this together and review regularly.

Is everything I share confidential?

Yes, confidentiality is fundamental to therapy. Everything discussed remains private, with very few exceptions (such as risk of harm). This will be explained fully in our first session.

What is integrative therapy?

Integrative therapy draws from various therapeutic approaches to tailor the work to your individual needs. Rather than following one model, I adapt techniques from different frameworks to best support your unique journey.

How do I book an appointment?

Simply fill out the contact form above or email us directly at hello@hourglasstherapy.co.uk. We'll get back to you to arrange an initial consultation at a time that works for you.
