Our Location

Hourglass Therapy is based within the beautiful Penny Brohn UK centre in Pill, Bristol — a peaceful setting for your therapy journey.

About the Location

Our therapy room is located within Penny Brohn UK, a nationally recognised centre for integrative health and wellbeing. The centre sits on Chapel Pill Lane in the village of Pill, just a short distance from Bristol city centre.

Set within beautiful grounds overlooking the Avon Gorge, Penny Brohn UK provides a uniquely calming environment — the perfect backdrop for reflection, healing, and personal growth.

Getting Here

By Car

From M5 Junction 19, take the A369 towards Pill. Parking is available at the centre.

By Bus

The X3 and X4 bus services run from Bristol city centre to Pill village.

By Train

Bristol Temple Meads station, then bus to Pill. Alternatively, the Pill ferry operates from Bristol harbour.

Use postcode BS20 0HH for directions directly to the centre.
