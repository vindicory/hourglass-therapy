Hypnotherapy Details

What Is Hypnotherapy?

Hypnotherapy can help you break free from unhelpful habits, manage anxiety, reduce chronic pain, and overcome phobias – all in a relaxed, focused state.

Contrary to common misconceptions, you remain in control at all times, simply accessing a state of heightened focus and deep calm.

What to Expect

Sessions involve a combination of relaxation techniques, guided imagery, and conversational hypnosis, tailored to your needs and goals. Each session is designed to help you access your inner resources and create lasting positive change.

Integrated Therapy

Hypnotherapy can be offered as a standalone service or blended into counselling, depending on your preference. This integrated approach allows us to address both conscious and subconscious patterns for more comprehensive support.

Hypnotherapy Can Support

  • Building confidence and self-esteem

    Reprogram limiting beliefs and build inner strength

  • Improving sleep

    Address insomnia and establish healthier sleep patterns

  • Managing chronic pain

    Learn techniques to reduce pain perception and improve quality of life

  • Overcoming fears and phobias

    Release irrational fears and regain freedom

  • Reducing anxiety, stress, and panic

    Access deep relaxation and develop coping strategies

  • Smoking cessation

    Break free from nicotine dependency for good
